# k=2 explicit example (Erdős–Rosenfeld factorization method)

Differences: 16 and 26.

- N1 = 192
  - 4*192+16^2 = 1024 = 32^2
  - 4*192+26^2 = 1444 = 38^2
- N2 = 2640
  - 4*2640+16^2 = 10816 = 104^2
  - 4*2640+26^2 = 11236 = 106^2

Construction: α=13, β=8, (α-β)(α+β)=105=3*5*7.
Factorization 3*35 gives (x,y)=(19,16), M=x^2-α^2=192.
Factorization 1*105 gives (x,y)=(53,52), M=2640.

Verified by `compute/factor_diff.py` logic: 16,26 ∈ D(192)∩D(2640).
